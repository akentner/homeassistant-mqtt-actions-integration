## Decisions this plan implements (visible, not silent)

- **D-15 and Phase 9 D-04 with its amendment (owner-approved, v1.0.1 only):** the owner pushes main first, with the refspec of FIX, and then dispatches both workflows. CI evidence counts with event `push` or `workflow_dispatch`, and headSha must equal FIX exactly. Claude checks with `gh run list` and never runs `gh workflow run` or `gh run rerun`.
- **D-14:** only FIX and its ancestors reach origin/main here. The later `.planning` commits of local main may be pushed by the owner after the tag and are not a gate.
- **Reversibility:** the push of main is rated `costly`. Undoing it would need a force push of the public main. It is the owner's human action, so no extra decision checkpoint is inserted. The one-way door (the tag, D-13) stays in plan 09-04, which has its own checkpoint:decision.
