## Shared Patterns

### Fail-safe around registry steps
**Source:** `manager.py:849-857` (`_remove_replay_twins`). Apply to the content read + clear in the catch.
`except Exception` -> `LOGGER.warning(<count-free text>)` + `LOGGER.debug("... failed with %s", type(err).__name__)`.

### Log hygiene
Counts only, never ids/names/payloads (e.g. `takeover.py:246`). New log lines for kept rows (D-03) carry a count.

### Retained clear via publisher
**Source:** `discovery.py:215-217` `async_clear_device` (publishes `""`, `retain=True` on the core prefix) wrapped by
`_async_attempt` (`manager.py:374-381`). Never raw `gateway.async_publish` in the manager.

### Owner rule
`device_id in self.devices and device_id not in self.sync.transferred_away` (`manager.py:1117`, `sync.py:366-369`,
`sync.py:775`). Followers: product publishes nothing; core's own `None` hook publish is accepted (D-02).

### Synchronous registry decision (WR-01)
No `await` between `sync.discovery_content(...)` and `remove_replay_twins`; flush afterwards via
`takeover.async_flush_registries` (`manager.py:885`).

### Test assertions on publishes
`_discovery_clears` (product `""`) vs. `_falsy_publishes` (includes core `None`), `tests/test_takeover.py:153-160`.
