<objective>
Close gap G-01 (ledger finding F-01): the local failure issue of a mirror must stay until its trigger succeeds or its customization goes. Today it is created non-persistent, so Home Assistant reloads it inactive with `data=None` after a restart. On top of that, a transient prune and a failed adoption delete it through the generic per-device issue cleanup while the record stays.

Purpose: D-19 and MIR-13 promise that only a proven success of the same trigger or removal of its customization clears the issue (T-11-21). The restart and prune paths break that promise silently.
Output: a persistent local issue; prune, unload and drop keep it; a saved tombstone discard and a completed adoption delete it; the start deletes an issue whose record is gone; corrected troubleshooting text; ledger row F-01 FIXED.

The ledger lists four files for G-01. The code shows a fifth one: `tests/test_local_actions_manager.py` pins the non-persistent flag in `test_local_failure_raises_an_independent_issue_naming_the_trigger_and_error`. That flag is exactly what G-01 changes, so this plan flips that one assertion. No other existing assertion changes. The regression test id stays the one the ledger names.
</objective>
