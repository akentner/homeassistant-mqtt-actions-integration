## Phase Goal

All fourteen checks have a recorded result with evidence on the final build, every failure is raised as a gap, and the archived Phase 1 to 3 reports are refreshed to `passed` where all their items passed (ROADMAP Phase 8 success criteria 5 and 6; decisions D-05, D-06, D-07).

<objective>
Finalize `08-UAT.md`, prove nothing recorded is stale, take a secret-free copy of the bench scripts, document the bench state left behind, run the verifier for Phases 1 to 3 with the ledger as evidence, and gate the result with `verification status`.

Purpose: a phase of manual checks is only as good as its ledger. This plan checks the ledger as a whole (no pending, no hole, gaps for every failure), re-asserts D-03, and then closes the Phase 1 to 3 human-verification items through the verifier, the way Phase 7 did for Phase 4 (never by hand-editing frontmatter).

Output: a complete ledger, requirement verdicts, an empty stale anchor, regenerated archived reports for Phases 1 to 3, and one final gate.
</objective>
