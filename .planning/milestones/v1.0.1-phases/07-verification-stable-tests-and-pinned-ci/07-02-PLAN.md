## Phase Goal

The v1.0.1 build is verifiably sound: the test suite is deterministic, and `test_delete_device_own_subscription_never_sees_state_clear` no longer flakes because its cause is fixed, not because it is retried or skipped (QLT-02). This plan diagnoses the cause, removes the whole defect class and proves it under load. The three consecutive full-suite runs are plan 07-03, because they must run on the combined head of plans 07-01 and 07-02.

<objective>
Fix the cause of the known flake and of its two identical siblings, and prove it. The cause was established in research and is re-captured here by a permanent reproducer: an autouse fixture that makes asyncio's slow-callback WARNING appear in every test. With it on and the assertions unscoped, exactly two tests fail; scoping their record counts to the integration's own loggers makes the suite green with the noise always on. The named test was already scoped in Phase 5 (it uses `own_warnings`); the two other tests had the same defect. A last task proves 50 consecutive error-free repetitions of the named test under load.

Purpose: a test that asserts the absence of a foreign record passes alone (25 of 25 isolated runs during the discussion) and fails once in a while in a full run on a loaded machine. Making the noise constant turns that into a deterministic red, so no assertion can depend on it again, instead of a retry that hides it.

Output: the always-slow fixture in `tests/conftest.py`, scoped assertions in three test modules, the proof ledger `07-PROOF.md` started with the stress result. No change under `custom_components/`.
</objective>

## Decisions this plan implements (visible, not silent)

- **D-05 (diagnose before fixing):** research reproduced the defect deterministically in a scratch copy of HEAD (always-slow loop: 2 failed, 1461 passed in 30 s; after scoping: 1463 passed; broker tier 17 passed with the probe on). Task 1 captures that evidence again in the real tree before it fixes anything: the failing assertion lines and the asyncio record text go into the SUMMARY. The race candidate named in CONTEXT (subscription removal ordering in `_async_remove_device`, `manager.py` line 2126) is not involved: the named test passes under the probe and the two failing tests never touch it, so no production change is made.
- **D-06 (fix where the cause is):** the cause is a test-scope defect, so the tests become deterministic by scoping what they count. No retry decorator, no skip, no expected-failure marker, no rerun plugin, no `sleep`, and no global silencing of the asyncio logger (that would hide a real signal in every test).
- **D-07 (TDD):** the race cannot be forced in production code because there is none, but the test-scope defect can: the always-slow fixture is the deterministic reproducer, committed red first (`test(07-02)`) and green after the fix (`fix(07-02)`).
- **D-08 (stress proof):** 50 consecutive error-free repetitions of the named test under load, on top of the three-run proof of plan 07-03. The load is four busy loops sharing two cores with the test (research: 50 passes under 16 busy loops, unit tier green under 2-core starvation). Task 3 runs it right after the fix, so a surviving flake is found in wave 1.
- **Slow-loop probe is permanent (planner decision on research open question 5, Claude's discretion):** an autouse fixture in `tests/conftest.py`, not an environment-gated probe. Captured logs print only for failing tests, so the noise costs nothing in a green run, and it is the strongest guarantee that no later test depends on the absence of the foreign record.
- **Shell loop instead of `pytest-repeat` (research):** the package was rated suspicious by the legitimacy check and the project adds nothing beyond PHACC; a bash loop costs about six minutes and `--keep-duplicates` does not repeat a node id.
